# Problem and solution

## Problem statement

Recent serious incidents in and around the USM campus have made many students, especially women, feel unsafe moving around at night. Hit and run accidents and dogs chasing students are also real risks. Today:

- The official report process usually takes a few days to respond, so security cannot act while an incident is still happening.
- Official statements come late, so students and staff often hear about danger through friends instead of a trusted source.
- There is no single place where everyone can see where incidents happened.
- Students have no quick way to share their journey with someone they trust or to call for help with their location.

## Solution

SafeWalk is a live, centralized safety system for USM:

- Students share a trip with trusted contacts. If they do not arrive on time, contacts and security are alerted with the last known location.
- Anyone in danger can call for help in one tap, and report while it is happening or after.
- All students and staff can see verified incident locations on one map.
- Security staff get a live dashboard, can track each case, and can send official advisories.
